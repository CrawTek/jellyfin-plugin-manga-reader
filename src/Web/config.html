Manga Reader

Read CBZ and ZIP manga from your Books libraries. Each Jellyfin account keeps its own reading progress on the server.

Open Manga Reader

Share the reader link with your users. They sign in with their own Jellyfin accounts. Optionally add the link to Jellyfin Web's custom menu links.

Version 0.1 supports CBZ/ZIP containing JPEG, PNG, WebP, GIF, or AVIF images. CBR, PDF, EPUB, and native TV clients are not supported in this release.
